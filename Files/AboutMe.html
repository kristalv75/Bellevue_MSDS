About Me

In November 2019, just before the COVID pandemic hit, I was diagnosed with stage 3 Rhabdomayosarcoma, and in that time, I took a step back to reevaluate what I wanted to do with the rest of my borrowed time on this earth. I have always had a love for data and analytics, which is why, as of the time of writing this, I am mere weeks from completing my M.S. in Data Science. Having a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering help lay the technical foundations of my successes.

I am a passionate and creative problem solver cursed with the "I figure it out" gene. In my spare time I enjoy powerlifting, cuddling with my three dogs, playing video games, and delving in all sorts of arts and crafts.
